THE WORKSPACE

Stay oriented, even when
the work branches out.

Zurafa brings the moving parts of terminal work into one coherent place.

01 / STRUCTURE

Sessions with room to think.

Organize work into sessions. Open tabs, split panes, focus what matters, and zoom a pane when you need every pixel.

WorkspaceTab 01 ╱ Tab 02Pane A │ Pane B
02 / REMOTE
⌁

Go remote with SSH.

Save hosts and open remote sessions through the system SSH client. Your existing keys, aliases, and host verification stay in play.

03 / SIGNAL
working done blocked

See structured status.

OSC 7501 reports let compatible programs share progress and state without Zurafa guessing from terminal text.

04 / YOUR SETUP
◐

Bring your terminal taste.

Import Ghostty colors and fonts, follow your system appearance, and add an optional dithered background image.

05 / UNDER THE HOOD
↺

Built around real state.

A Rust daemon owns sessions, layout, PTYs, events, and snapshots. The macOS app follows that state and can recover after reconnecting.
